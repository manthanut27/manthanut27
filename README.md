## 🧭 About

I'm a final-year BCA (Computer Applications) student at Sahyog College of IT & Management Studies, affiliated with the University of Mumbai (Class of 2027), building software that ships and runs in production — not tutorial clones. My focus is full-stack engineering with a deliberate lean toward AI-integrated products, and I care as much about where AI *shouldn't* sit in a system as where it should.

Recent work spans a live e-commerce platform processing real payments end-to-end, a fully-tested AI-augmented auditing engine built under assessment conditions, and applied coursework in agentic systems. My default is deterministic logic for anything decision-critical — LLMs get used specifically for summarization, vision, and natural-language interfaces, never as a substitute for engineering judgment.
